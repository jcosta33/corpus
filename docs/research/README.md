# Research

Corpus's evidence lives in two kinds of documents.

- **The bibliography.** [sources.md](sources.md) is the bibliography for product prose: every
  `[[KEY]]` citation resolves there. [`skills/sources.md`](../../skills/sources.md) grounds how the
  artifact authors are written.
- **Dated research records.** These are the studies and tests behind recent decisions. They describe
  the state on their date and are not current product documentation. Current truth lives in the
  [method](../method.md), the [formats](../formats.md), and the [ADR ledger](../adrs/README.md).
